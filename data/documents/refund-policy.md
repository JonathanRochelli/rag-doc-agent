# Refund Policy — NovaTrack

## General principle

NovaTrack applies a 30-day refund policy on all paid plans (Solo and Team). Any request made within 30 days of a payment is refunded in full, no justification required.

## How to request a refund

Requests go through the support contact form (support@novatrack.example), stating the account email and the date of the payment in question. Refunds are processed within 5 business days and appear on the bank statement within an additional 10 business days, depending on the bank.

## Special cases

- **Missed automatic renewal**: if a customer reports an automatic renewal they meant to cancel, within 30 days of the charge, the refund is granted automatically.
- **Enterprise plan**: refund terms are defined contractually on a case-by-case basis and do not follow the standard 30-day policy.
- **Detected abuse**: NovaTrack reserves the right to deny a refund in cases of repeated, abusive requests on the same account (more than 2 refunds within a rolling 12 months).

## Cancellation without a refund

A customer can cancel their subscription at any time without requesting a refund: access to paid features stays active until the end of the period already paid for, and the account then automatically drops to the free Solo plan.
